About Us

Your link to The Highest Quality

AJ Link is a Client-oriented, Miami based, hotel and restaurant supplier with additional offices located in Venezuela, Panama, Chile and Peru. With over a decade of international experience, we have the necessary structure and knowledge to coordinate and facilitate any hotel or restaurant installation.

We also offer engineering, architecture and interior design consulting services to the hospitality industry. Our privileged relationships with the most reputable hotel equipment and supply manufacturers enable us to provide extremely competitive prices on all the supplies you demand.

More Than Just a Voice on The Phone

AJ LINK's welcoming, knowledgeable and professional staff will help inspire, educate and problem-solve for you. Clients today want the very most and the very best for the very least amount of money, and on the best terms. Only the companies that provide absolutely excellent supplies and services at absolutely excellent prices will survive. We have not only survived, but thrived for over a decade doing just that.

We provide product procurement and logistic services for the expansion, equipping and remodeling of existing facilities, as well as new hospitality projects, mainly in the Caribbean and Latin America. We also enjoy keeping our Clients well-informed on state-of-the-art technologies and Eco-friendly solutions. It's our job every day to make every important aspect of our Client’s experience a little bit better.
